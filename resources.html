Planning and learning

Coaching and session planning

Good sessions come from good source material. These resources cover most of what a club coach or a helping parent needs, and none of them assume prior experience.

Get into coaching

  • World Athletics Kids’ Athletics

    Free games and activities for 4–14 year olds, built around movement skills rather than event technique. Used by more than 150 member federations.

    Programme

  • āhei

    Ready-made session plans that work for mixed-ability groups, free to New Zealand coaches and teachers.

    Planning

  • ASB Get Set Go

    Fundamental movement skills for 3–8 year olds, with skill cards, session planners and videos. The foundation our youngest groups are built on. Accessible to NCAN coaches through āhei.

    Ages 3–8

  • ASB Run Jump Throw

    Athletics-specific skills for 6–11 year olds using modified equipment and games, teaching basic movement patterns before refined technique. Accessible to NCAN coaches through āhei.

    Ages 6–11

  • Balance is Better

    Sport NZ’s guidance on keeping young people in sport — realistic expectations, late developers, and what helps rather than hinders. Written for coaches and parents alike.

    Guide

Entries and results

Athletic.net

Athletics Canterbury and Athletics NZ take entries through Athletic.net for most events and publish results through AthleticLIVE. Everyone who competes needs an account, and children aged 12 and under are entered by a parent or caregiver from theirs.

Before your first entry

  • Set the account up on a computer

    Create it on a laptop, PC or tablet. Once it exists, a phone is fine for entering meetings.

  • Children 12 and under

    They sit under a parent or caregiver’s account, and that adult enters the events and pays. Older children can be linked the same way if it suits you better.

  • Claim your bio

    Anyone who has competed in recent seasons already has results on Athletic.net. Claim that bio, and merge any duplicates so everything sits under one login.

  • Enter an event

    Open the event/meeting’s own info page, go to the Register tab, and follow the prompts for each event you are entering.
