Planning and learning

Coaching and session planning

Good sessions come from good source material. These four cover most of what a club coach or a helping parent needs, and none of them assume prior experience.

Get into coaching

  • World Athletics Kids’ Athletics

    Free games and activities for 4–14 year olds, built around movement skills rather than event technique. Used by more than 150 member federations.

    Programme

  • āhei

    Ready-made session plans that work for mixed-ability groups, free to New Zealand coaches and teachers.

    Planning

  • ASB Get Set Go

    Fundamental movement skills for 3–8 year olds, with skill cards, session planners and videos. The foundation our youngest groups are built on. Accessible to NCAN coaches through āhei.

    Ages 3–8

  • ASB Run Jump Throw

    Athletics-specific skills for 6–11 year olds using modified equipment and games, teaching basic movement patterns before refined technique. Accessible to NCAN coaches through āhei.

    Ages 6–11

  • Balance is Better

    Sport NZ’s guidance on keeping young people in sport — realistic expectations, late developers, and what helps rather than hinders. Written for coaches and parents alike.

    Guide

How it is run

Rules and governing bodies

The rule book, e-learning platform and the two organisations NCAN is affiliated to.

Become an official

  • World Athletics Book of Rules

    The full rule book. Officials want C2.1 Technical Rules, under Book C, which covers every track and field event.

    Rules

  • Athletics NZ e-learning portal

    Courses, past webinars, and a library split into club, officials and coaching resources. Sign in with a World Athletics account the first time.

    Learning

  • Athletics Canterbury

    Our regional body: the competition calendar, interclub and championship results, records and rankings, and officials and volunteer pathways.

    Regional

  • Athletics New Zealand

    Our national body, and the source of the programmes, courses and membership rules the club runs under.

    National
