Why NCAN

More than a season of Saturdays

Athletics is not just running, jumping, and throwing. It's also how to turn up, set goals, keep going when the last lap hurts, and most of all have fun with your friends. Our job is to make that experience welcoming enough that people stay for decades, not just a summer.

Local, and proud of it

Rangiora, Kaiapoi, Woodend, Oxford, Amberley, Waikuku and beyond – one club, training close to home.

Every ability, genuinely

First timers train alongside our competition squads, with the same coaches and the same welcome.

A pathway that keeps going

From children’s athletics to Canterbury representation and masters competition, with coaching support at every step.

Four entry points, one club. Not sure where you or your child belongs? Come to a Tuesday club night or a Monday training and try it before you register.

Ages 7–14

Junior athletics

For children age 7 to 14. Weekly club nights covering sprints, distance, long jump, high jump, shot put, discus, race walking and hurdles – taught as play, fun for everyone.

Competition opportunities for Saturday Interclub, Colgate Games and Canterbury Championships.

Tuesday 5:15pm · Oct–Mar

Teen development,
Seniors & Masters

Event-group squads with qualified coaches, and individualised training. Access to specialist equipment and a chance to really develop your skills.

Coaching support to help you reach your competition goals.

Tuesday 6:30pm · Oct–Mar

Running and race walking

Runners & Race Walkers

Just into running or want to explore race walking?

We offer cross country and road racing training including race walking Apr–Sep and track event training including sprints, distance, race walks and hurdles Oct–Mar. Suitable for ages 10 through to masters athletes.

Monday 5:30pm · All year

Pathways

Coaching & officials pathways

Athletics NZ accredited coach courses, officials training, and support for athletes stepping up to Canterbury and national championships.

Suitable for everyone. From parents looking to support their child, athletes looking to better understand the rules and regulations, and aspiring coaches and officials.

    Steps 2 and 3 are both completed in the GameDay portal. A link will be sent to you after completing the Google form.

    1. 2

      Select your membership option
