Results and progression

Our athletes

Every North Canterbury athlete with published track and field results, each with a page charting their progression in every event, for the club and for any team they represented while a member of NCAN. Cross country and road events aren’t included. Results are for club families, coaches and the committee: sign in to see them. Families see their own children.

These pages draw mainly on AthleticLIVE (athletic.net), where Athletics Canterbury, school and national meetings have published results since 2024. Results from before then, and athletes who only competed before then, are missing. The club would love to hear about any sources for missing or earlier results.

Athletes

Athletes are listed under the most recent season they competed in, with their age group that season: grade 7–14 for children (younger children run in grade 7), then U16–U20, Senior and Masters as Athletics NZ’s competition rules define them, by age on 31 December. Pages are built from results already published by the meetings, so a result can take a few days to appear.

Your page

Rather your page not be shown?

Whether it’s your own page or your child’s, tell us and we will remove it, as we do for photos. Athletes 16 and over can ask for themselves; for younger athletes, a parent or caregiver. If a result is missing or wrong, let us know that too.
