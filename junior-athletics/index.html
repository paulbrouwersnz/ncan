Junior club night, ages 7–14

Junior Athletics

Tuesday club nights for children aged 7 to 14, 5:00–6:15pm at the Rangiora New Life School field, October to March. Each session’s plan shows what every group does, time slot by time slot. If a session is cancelled, it says so here, on the North Canterbury Children’s Athletics Facebook page and in the club’s other messages to families.

High school, senior and masters athletes can train from 6:00pm. Please see a coach or committee member to be cleared first.

How to use the planner

Start a plan

  • New training plan starts from the usual club night: warm up and set up at 5:00, three empty slots, the long run or race walk and pack down at 6:00, and the note to parents.
  • Start from the latest plan copies the most recent plan to the next Tuesday.
  • Pick the session date with the arrows (a week at a time) or the calendar. Changing the date of a saved plan moves it, and its share link follows.

Put groups together

  • Tap chips to pick them: grades, who (Girls, Boys) and activities. Then tap each group they go on, or a time slot’s New group. Tap Done when finished. On a computer you can drag a chip onto a group instead.
  • Pick several at once: e.g. Grade 7, Grade 8, Girls and Long jump make one group in one tap.
  • Girls & Boys picks Girls and Boys; All athletes picks every grade, Girls and Boys. Parents & guardians is a group of its own, for setting up and packing down.
  • A group can do up to four activities in a time slot, e.g. Starts then Sprints. Not in the list? Type it in Another activity.
  • In a group: × takes a grade or activity out, the drop-down changes girls, boys or both, and Move or Copy then a tap puts the block somewhere else.
  • Time slots: change the start (the end moves with it) or the end; Copy slot repeats one straight after; Add a time slot adds one at the end.

Check it

  • An amber outline and a note under the slot mean two groups are at the same station, or the same athletes are in two groups at once. You can still save if that’s what you meant.

Notes

  • Notes go on a group, a time slot, an activity (for the whole session, e.g. the shot put weights) or the whole session.
  • The shaded coaches-only boxes are seen only by coaches, the committee and admins, signed in, and on Print with coaches’ notes. Families never see them.

Save and release

  • Save as a draft: only signed-in coaches, the committee and admins see it.
  • Release (or Save and release) puts it on this page for families. Back to draft takes it off again while you change it.
  • Schedule release releases a draft by itself at a set time, e.g. the evening before. Until then families see when it will be released.
  • Cancel the session asks why; families see the reason. If the plan had been released, they can still look at it with Show the plan. Reinstate as a draft undoes a cancellation.
  • Copy to a new date starts a new plan from this one; Delete removes it (a share link then says it was removed).
  • Only the soonest upcoming plan shows in full. A later one shows its buttons but not the plan until you tap Show the plan (its print buttons work either way).

Share and print

  • Share link gives a link to a released or cancelled plan, read-only, without coaches’ notes, for messages and Facebook.
  • Print plan, Print with coaches’ notes, or Print strips for each group to cut up and hand out. Printed plans (not strips) end with a QR code to the plan online.
  • Tap a group on a plan to light up its whole session, or activities in the strip below it to see who does them. Print selected then prints just those.
