‼️FREE CERTIFIED RACE WALK JUDGING COURSE IN CHRISTCHURCH‼️

Canterbury and Athletics NZ are holding a level 1 Race Walk judging course at Nga Puna Wai on Wednesday the 12th of August, 6pm - 9pm.

Excellent opportunity to learn A LOT about race walking technique and exactly how judging works. After the course, you will be certified to judge at certain events.

The test is completely open book, and FREE for anyone to take part in. I would highly recommend this to people who are interested in judging, or to new race walkers who will gain valuable info with race walking technique and understanding the sport more. I did this course 10 years ago, and it helped me a lot - not only as a judge, but as an athlete and coach as well.

Please send me a message or text if you wish to attend!
Bring your family members too!
