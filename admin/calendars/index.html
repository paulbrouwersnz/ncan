Coaches’ tools

Calendars

The club’s calendars: add, change or cancel events, and choose which calendars each one shows in. Coaches, the committee and administrators can edit events; administrators can also make and change the calendars themselves. Everyone sees them on the calendar page, and phones that subscribe get each change.

Loading…

Saved changes show on the calendar page within a minute. Phones that subscribe get them at the next automatic publish, usually within 30 minutes, then when their calendar app next checks (Google Calendar can take up to a day).
