The website

Header photos

The photos behind the green band at the top of the site’s pages. Put approved photos in pools, then choose which pages use which pool, down to one address such as a single training plan (/junior-athletics/#/plan/2026-11-24); a page shows one of its photos at random on each visit. Photos must be on photos.ncan.nz. Only put photos of children in a pool for public pages when the club has consent to publish them.
