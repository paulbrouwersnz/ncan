From GameDay

Members list

The members list decides who has a page, and which seasons’ results count for the club. Update it from two GameDay exports whenever memberships change. Nothing changes until you’ve seen the check and pressed Import.

Where to get the two files
  1. Sign in to GameDay (admin.mygameday.app) as the club.
  2. Go to Reporting, then Tabular Reports.
  3. Open Advanced Member. Choose Export, set it to All rows and All columns, and export as CSV. No need to change the page’s filters.
  4. Do the same with Transactions: All rows and All columns, with no date filter, so it holds every season’s memberships.
  5. The files download straight away, or GameDay shows a notification when they’re ready. Choose them below.

The files hold members’ personal details. They go only to the club’s server, and are deleted there once imported (or after three hours). Delete your downloaded copies afterwards.

On top of the GameDay list

Membership changes

The members list comes from the club’s membership system (GameDay), and a result counts for the club only while the athlete was a member. Changes here sit on top of that list, so the next import doesn’t undo them.

athletic.net numbers

Merges

AthleticLIVE sometimes gives one athlete a second athletic.net number, after a change of school or club, or at a school meeting. The collector joins them into one page when their grades match, or when one member has that name; a person can join or separate any. Each number’s latest results show what’s under it.

Made on the website

Corrections

Pages removed at a family’s request, athletes whose two numbers were joined (or kept apart), and names a meeting spells differently. Undo puts things back at the next collect.

Sign-in

Blocked from the whole site

A blocked email can’t sign in, whatever the membership system says, and is signed out at once. To stop someone seeing one athlete only, use that athlete’s page.
