Behind the athlete pages

Results sources

Every meeting the athlete pages are built from, with what each one added. Most come from AthleticLIVE (athletic.net); a few, such as primary school sports, were typed in by hand from the results the organisers published.

Know of a meeting that’s missing, or earlier results? The club would love to hear about it.

For coaches and the committee

Add a meeting

Open the meeting on AthleticLIVE, add /teams to its address, choose a club that has results (North Canterbury if it competed; any club works, as the whole meeting is read) and copy that page’s address. Add a meeting once its results are complete: what’s published when it’s collected is kept.
