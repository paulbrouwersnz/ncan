Join us for our Monday training sessions

Monday Training

Monday distance running and sprint specific training sessions are held at Ashgrove Park, Kingsbury Ave, Rangiora. All are welcome to join us for a run or sprint session, and we encourage you to come along and give it a try. Communications about changes to training sessions are made via our Facebook messenger group so please get in touch if you would like to be added to the group. We look forward to seeing you at training!
