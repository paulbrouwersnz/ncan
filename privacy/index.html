Your information

Privacy

The club keeps a little information about its members, most of them children, so we can run the club and show families their results. Here is what we hold, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how to ask about it. We follow the Privacy Act 2020.

Ask about your information What we hold

What and why

What we hold

Only what we need. Most of it comes from your membership registration in GameDay, the membership system Athletics New Zealand clubs use; results come from meetings that are already published.

Membership details
Name, date of birth, gender, the seasons you were a member, and your registration type with the club, Athletics Canterbury and Athletics New Zealand. We use them to know who is a member, to put athletes in the right grade, and to know which results count for the club. Nothing else from GameDay is kept: no addresses, phone numbers or payment details.
Email addresses
The member’s own, the parents’ or guardians’, and the person who paid, as given in GameDay. They are used for one thing only: sending a sign-in link when you ask for one, so you can see your own athlete’s results page. No newsletters, no marketing, and never passed on.
Results
Marks and placings from meetings whose results are already published: AthleticLIVE, Athletics Canterbury, and school and road race results. They make up each athlete’s results page, the club championships and the club records.
Signing in
When you sign in, we keep a record that your device is signed in, until it runs out (90 days) or you sign out. Sign-in links work once, for 30 minutes.
Changes we make
When a club administrator corrects something (joins two results records that are one athlete, changes a membership), we keep who did it and when. Any note about why is cleared after two years.

Who sees it

Who can see what

Athlete results pages aren’t public. Dates of birth, registrations and email addresses are seen only by the club’s administrators.

Athlete results pages
A family signs in to see their own athletes’ pages. The club’s coaches and committee see everyone’s.
Club administrators
See the members list, with dates of birth, registrations and the emails that can sign in, to keep it right. Only a few committee members are administrators.
Club championships
The children’s club championships page is public. It shows each athlete’s first name and the first letter of their surname; full names show only when you’re signed in.
Club records
The records page names the record holders, as club records always have.
Nobody else
We don’t sell, swap or pass on members’ information. Registration with Athletics Canterbury and Athletics New Zealand happens in GameDay, under their own privacy terms.

Where and how long

How we keep it

On the club’s own server
The information is kept on a server the club runs in New Zealand, reached only over a secure connection, and its backups are kept as private as the server. Sign-in emails are sent through Gmail (Google), which sends them for the club and does nothing else with them.
While you’re a member
We keep what’s above while you’re a member, and for the season after.
After you leave
Once someone hasn’t been a member this season or last, their date of birth, gender and registrations are removed, and their family’s emails can no longer sign in. We keep only their name, birth year and the seasons they were a member, so their results stay with the right person in the club’s history.
The GameDay files
When an administrator updates the members list from GameDay, the files are deleted from the server once they’re read, or after three hours.

Your rights

Ask about your information

You can ask what we hold about you or your child, and ask us to correct it or remove it. Ask the club’s privacy officer.

Contact the privacy officer
Privacy officer
Paul Brouwers, on the committee.
See what we hold
We’ll send you everything we hold about you or your child within 20 working days.
Correct it
Membership details are corrected in GameDay or by us; results by us. If we don’t agree a correction is needed, we’ll keep your statement with your record, so it’s always seen with it.
Remove it
We’ll remove your child’s results page, or hold back their name from the club championships page, if you ask. We can do the same for photos in the gallery.
Not happy with our answer?
You can complain to the Privacy Commissioner.
