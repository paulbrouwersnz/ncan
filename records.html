Children’s club records

The best performance by a North Canterbury athlete in each event, at each grade. Where we hold a link to the official results, the date links to it so the record can be checked.

The record book

Records by grade

Grade is the athlete’s age on 31 December. A record stands until it is beaten, so where two athletes share a mark both are listed. Records are generally updated at the end of each season, in late March or early April.

Keep it accurate

Spotted something wrong?

These go back to 2005 and some of the older entries came from paper. If you know a record is out of date, misspelled, or you can point us at the official results for one, please tell us and we will fix it.
