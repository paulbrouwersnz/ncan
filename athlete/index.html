All results

Newest first, or grouped by event. Each mark links to the official result. Place is the place in the whole race or competition, out of everyone who recorded a result.

These pages draw mainly on AthleticLIVE (athletic.net), where Athletics Canterbury, school and national meetings have published results since 2024. Results from before then, and athletes who only competed before then, are missing. The club would love to hear about any sources for missing or earlier results.

Out of Stadia

Cross country, road and relays

Newest first. Cross country times depend on the course and the conditions, so they aren’t compared as personal bests: the place in the race tells more. A relay shows the runner’s own leg and their team’s place. Grades are cross country and road grades: two-year junior grades by age on 31 December, and Masters by age on the day.

Where the results come from

Results sources

Every meeting these results come from, track and field, cross country, road and relays, newest first. Open one for the official results and what was done there. ✍️ marks a meeting entered manually from the results the organisers published; the rest come from AthleticLIVE.

Your page

Something missing, or rather not shown?

These pages are built from results already published by the meetings. If a result is missing or wrong, or you would rather your page – or your child’s – not be shown, tell us and we will fix or remove it. Athletes 16 and over can ask for themselves; for younger athletes, a parent or caregiver.

Sharing

Who can see this page

The contacts the club has in its membership system can see this page. You can let others see it too, such as a grandparent or another carer: add their email address, and they can sign in with it. Nobody is emailed when you add them. People you add can see the page, but can’t add anyone else.

Header photo

A photo for this page

A photo of the athlete behind the heading at the top of this page. Only the people who can see the page see it. The photo is cropped and made smaller here, on your device, which also leaves out where and when it was taken.

Drag the frame to choose the part to show, and zoom to make it smaller. Click on the athlete to keep them in view on a phone, where less of the photo shows. The heading at the top of the page shows how it will look.

For club administrators

Manage this athlete

The athletic.net numbers this page is made from. AthleticLIVE sometimes gives an athlete a second number, after a change of school or club, or at a school meeting. Check each against its athletic.net bio, add one that’s missing, or take out one that isn’t this athlete. Changes can be undone on the Club admin page.

Club memberships

Each season’s paid registrations in GameDay, as of the last members import: the club membership type, and their Athletics Canterbury and Athletics NZ registrations, each with the date it was ordered. Changes an admin made on the Club admin page are noted.
