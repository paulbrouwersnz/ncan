For coaches and the committee

Add a meeting

Open the meeting on AthleticLIVE and copy its address (e.g. …/meets/80234): a team with results is chosen for you, North Canterbury’s if it competed (the whole meeting is read either way). A team page’s address (…/meets/80234/teams/1855906) works too, if you’d rather choose. Add a meeting once its results are complete: what’s published when it’s collected is kept. Choose the type Cross country or Road for an out-of-stadia meeting, including cross country and road relays: those results are shown in a section of their own on athletes’ pages (each runner’s relay leg included), apart from track and field.

Name it in the club’s style, so the list reads consistently. The AthleticLIVE tab’s title shows its name to start from: tidy the spelling and punctuation.

  • Interclub rounds: by series and round, with no year (the dates tell seasons apart): “Canterbury Children’s Interclub, Programme 4”, “AC Senior Interclub 3”
  • Twilights: number, then year: “AC Twilight 1, 2026”
  • Championships, games and one-off meetings: the full name with the year: “Canterbury Children’s Championships 2026”, “NZ Track and Field Championships 2026”, “South Island Colgate Games 2026”
  • One day of a longer meeting: add it after a comma: “Canterbury Secondary Schools Championships 2026, Day 1”
  • Use AC for Athletics Canterbury, NZ for New Zealand, and full words otherwise.
