Adding the calendar to your own app

Subscribing is better than downloading: a subscription updates itself when we change a time or add a fixture, where a download is a snapshot of today.

  • iPhone and iPad

    Settings › Apps › Calendar › Accounts › Add Account › Other › Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the calendar address.

  • Android and Google Calendar

    On a computer, open Google Calendar, then Other calendars › From URL, and paste the address. It syncs to your phone.

  • Outlook

    Add calendar › Subscribe from web, then paste the address.

  • One-off download

    Use the button at the top of this page to grab the file and import it. It will not update later.
