Open Day

Open Days are Tuesday evening, October 6th and 13th at the Rangiora New Life School field. 5:00pm to 6:15pm for 7–14 year old children, and 6:00pm to 7:00pm for high school, senior and masters athletes. Come along and try out athletics with us!
