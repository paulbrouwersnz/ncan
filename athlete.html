Results sources

These pages draw mainly on AthleticLIVE (athletic.net), where Athletics Canterbury, school and national meetings have published results since 2024. Results from before then, and athletes who only competed before then, are missing. The club would love to hear about any sources for missing or earlier results.

Your page

Something missing, or rather not shown?

These pages are built from results already published by the meetings. If a result is missing or wrong, or you would rather your page – or your child’s – not be shown, tell us and we will fix or remove it. Athletes 16 and over can ask for themselves; for younger athletes, a parent or caregiver.
